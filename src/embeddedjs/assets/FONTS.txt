Fonts in this directory

Segment7.ttf  (source for the time, seconds and date fonts)
  "7-Segment" by Jan Bobrowski, https://torinak.com/font/7-segment
  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see Segment7-LICENSE.txt), unmodified.

Segment7Px72.ttf, Segment7Px44.ttf, Segment7Px24.ttf  (time, date, seconds)
  Modified versions of Segment7.ttf (SIL OFL 1.1), one per pixel size, made by
  tools/make_seg7.sh so the digits do not shift by a pixel when they change:
    tools/make_seg7.sh <python with fontTools>
